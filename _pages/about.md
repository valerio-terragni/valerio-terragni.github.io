I am a Senior Lecturer in the [Department of Electrical, Computer, and Software Engineering](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/engineering/about-the-faculty/electrical-computer-and-software-engineering.html) at [The University of Auckland](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en.html), New Zealand. The Senior Lecturer title is equivalent to **Associate Professor** in Asian, European, and North American universities. From 2017 to 2020, I was a Senior Research Fellow (post-doc) at the Faculty of Informatics at the [USI Università della Svizzera italiana](https://www.usi.ch/en), Lugano, Switzerland. In 2017, I obtained my PhD at [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://www.ust.hk/home), Hong Kong, advised by [Prof. S.C. Cheung](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~scc/). I received my B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Computer Science from [The University of Milano-BICOCCA](https://en.unimib.it/), Italy.

I am the **Program Director** of the Software Engineering Degree at the University of Auckland.

I am a Software Engineering researcher, my main research topics are:

- Software Testing
- SE4AI
- AI4SE
- Metamorphic Testing
- Automated est Input and Oracle Generation
- Machine Learning Testing
