Dr. Valerio Terragni

Senior Lecturer at The University of Auckland, New Zealand. ✉️v.terragni AT auckland DOT ac DOT nz

I am a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Electrical, Computer, and Software Engineering at The University of Auckland, New Zealand. The Senior Lecturer title is equivalent to Associate Professor in Asian, European, and North American universities. I was a Senior Research Fellow at the Faculty of Informatics, USI Università della Svizzera italiana, Lugano. I earned my PhD in 2017 from The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. S.C. Cheung. Earlier, I completed my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Computer Science at The University of Milano-BICOCCA, Italy.

I am the Program Director of the Software Engineering Degree at the University of Auckland.

My main research topics are:

  • Software Testing
  • SE4AI and AI4SE
  • Metamorphic Testing
  • Automated Input and Oracle Generation
  • Machine Learning Testing
  • Software Education
